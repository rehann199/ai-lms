# Privacy and Source

## What this repository is

A portfolio showcase for an application. It describes the engineering of AI-LMS; it is not a source distribution.

## What is published

- Written descriptions of architecture, design decisions and trade-offs
- Original diagrams created for this showcase
- Real screenshots from a local build

## What is intentionally not published

- Production source code, in whole or in part
- Environment files, API keys, tokens and service credentials
- Database contents and storage buckets
- Version control history of the source project
- Any real user, student or course data

## Screenshots

Screenshots are captures of a local build running on fictional demo data: invented people, courses, lecture text and accounts. AI responses shown were produced by a local stand-in for the language model, not by a hosted provider.

## Third-party components and licences

The app builds on open-source libraries (Next.js, React, Material UI, FastAPI, LangChain, LangGraph, sentence-transformers) and on hosted services (Supabase, OpenRouter, Together.ai). Their names and marks belong to their owners. The Inter typeface used in the showcase theme is loaded from Google Fonts under its open licence.

## How the descriptions were produced

The project was inspected read-only. Statements describe behaviour observed in code, manifests and project documents; anything not confirmed is worded conservatively or left out.
